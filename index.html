Lead Editorial

Data-Driven Astronomy Research Intern at Indian Space Academy

During my internship at the Indian Space Academy, I completed 120 hours of training in the Astronomy and Astrophysics Summer School 2025, focusing on data-driven approaches to modern cosmology. As part of my project work on Supernova Cosmology, I built a Lambda-CDM computational model using Type Ia supernovae data to estimate the Hubble constant and analyze the expansion history of the Universe, aligning results with late-universe (SH0ES) measurements.

The work involved developing and testing numerical pipelines in Python using tools such as NumPy, pandas, SciPy, Matplotlib, and Astropy, strengthening my understanding of how theoretical cosmology can be translated into computational models driven by real observational data.
